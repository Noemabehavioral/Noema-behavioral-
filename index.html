About

Meet Sophie

Sophie Nienburg, MA, BCBA, LBA

Founder & Behavioral Consultant

Board Certified Behavior Analyst and Licensed Behavior Analyst providing individualized behavioral consultation with a focus on practical, respectful, and meaningful support.

Sophie Nienburg is a Board Certified Behavior Analyst and Licensed Behavior Analyst dedicated to providing thoughtful, individualized, and evidence-based behavioral support.

Her approach begins with understanding each individual—their strengths, needs, environment, and the function of their behavior. Sophie works collaboratively with families, caregivers, educators, and other professionals to develop practical strategies that support meaningful skill development and lasting progress.

At Noema Behavioral Consulting, Sophie believes behavioral support should be compassionate, respectful, and applicable to everyday life. Her goal is to help individuals and the people who support them gain greater understanding, confidence, and the tools needed to create meaningful change.

Services

Individualized support with practical purpose.

Behavioral Consultation

Individualized consultation focused on understanding behavioral concerns, identifying meaningful goals, and developing practical, evidence-based recommendations tailored to each client and environment.

Parent & Caregiver Training

Collaborative coaching that helps families better understand behavior, strengthen important skills, and confidently use supportive strategies in everyday routines.

School Consultation

Noema partners with schools, educators, and multidisciplinary teams to develop individualized, evidence-based solutions that support student success. Consultation may include classroom observations, functional behavior assessment, behavior support plans, staff coaching, data-based decision-making, and recommendations that promote independence, participation, and long-term success.

Behavior Assessment & Intervention Planning

Function-based assessment and individualized intervention planning designed to identify why behavior occurs and guide effective, practical support.

Specialized Service

PFA / SBT

Practical Functional Assessment & Skill-Based Treatment

An individualized, compassionate approach to understanding and addressing challenging behavior, with emphasis on safety, trust, communication, tolerance, cooperation, and meaningful skill development.

Staff Training & Professional Consultation

Training, coaching, and consultation for educators, caregivers, and other professionals to support effective and consistent implementation of behavioral strategies.
